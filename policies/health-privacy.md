# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective [Effective date]**

This policy is for clients of Fiala Fitness who live in Washington or Nevada, or whose health data we collect while they're in either state. It explains how we handle your consumer health data, as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law define it.

## The health data we collect, and how we use it

**1. Health details, only after you agree,** in the app or, if you train in person and don't use the app, on a paper form. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian agrees too, after you: on your phone, or by signing the paper form with you.

- Your date of birth, sex, height and body weight.
- Your injuries and limits, medical conditions, medications and allergies, and whether you're pregnant.
- Your emergency contact and your doctor.

This covers what you enter in the app and what you tell your coach. We use them only for these purposes: to keep your training safe (your coach plans your workouts around them, sees your injuries and limits at the top of your workouts and your age under your name, and can call your emergency contact if something happens), and, from your date of birth, to know whether you're under 18 (so a parent or guardian agrees too) or under 13 (the app is for ages 13 and up). If health details were saved for you before [Effective date], we keep them until you answer: the app asks you the next time you open it (if your access is paused, once it's active again) whether to keep them, or you can answer on a paper form. Until you answer, your coach can see them but no one can change them, and if you tap Don't Allow, they're deleted.

**2. Reasons you pick in a workout.** "Sick" or "Injury" when you skip a workout, and "Fatigue" or "Discomfort" when you skip an exercise or finish it early. Giving a reason is optional. We use them to adjust your training. Your Progress page's Attendance card lists the reasons you gave for skipping workouts (you and your coach see it). Your coach's Progress page points out an exercise you cut short for discomfort on two or more days within four weeks, and a monthly report your coach sends you can list the reasons you gave for cutting an exercise short.

**3. Notes that mention your health,** such as a note on an exercise written by you or your coach ("knee sore on the last set"), and your coach's private notes about you. We use them to plan and adjust your training, and a monthly report your coach sends you can show your exercise notes.

**4. What we work out from the above:** your age and whether you're under 18, from your date of birth (if your health details are deleted while you're under 18, we keep only the year you turn 18), and the discomfort note on your coach's Progress page.

**5. Your training record.** Washington's law may also count as health data the fact that you're our client (your name, email address, phone number and photo), your plan, the workouts planned for you, what you log (weights, reps, times, distances and sets), your attendance and progress, the time zone saved with each finished workout, and your current time zone if you share it. We use it to provide the coaching you asked for: to plan and log your workouts, show you and your coach your progress, and make your monthly report.

**How we handle it.** It's kept in the app's database, which our database provider, Supabase, runs for us, and it's shown in the app only to you and your coach (your coach's private notes only to your coach). Copies are kept on your phone and on your coach's devices, so the app works without a signal, and in backup copies kept by Supabase and by your coach. If your coach sends you your monthly report, for example by email, your coach's email or messaging service carries it to you.

## Where it comes from

- **You,** when you enter it in the app or tell your coach.
- **Your coach,** who plans your workouts, writes notes, and enters health details you tell them once you've agreed.
- **Your parent or guardian,** if you're under 18 and they tell your coach about your health.
- **The app,** which reads your phone's time-zone setting and works out your age, whether you're under 18, and the discomfort note from the above.

## What we share, and with whom

We don't sell consumer health data, and we have no affiliates. Supabase stores it for us as our service provider and may use it only to run the app for us. Apart from that, we share it only in these cases, and only what's needed:

- **In an emergency,** with emergency services, your emergency contact or your doctor, to get you help: the health details they need, such as an injury, a condition, a medication, an allergy or a pregnancy.
- **When you ask us to,** with anyone you choose, such as your doctor, a physical therapist, or a parent or guardian: only what you give us separate written permission to share.
- **When the law requires it,** with a court or government authority, for example under a court order: only what the law requires.

## Tracking across other websites

No third party collects health data about you through our app, over time or across other websites or apps: the app has no advertising or analytics tools. The companies that deliver the app's files, typeface and code (GitHub, Google Fonts and jsDelivr), and YouTube when you open an exercise video, receive your device's internet address and browser details (YouTube also learns which video it is), but not your account, your workouts or your health details.

## Your rights, and how to use them

You can:

- **find out** whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and **get a copy** of it, with a list of everyone we've shared it with and how to contact them (we've never sold it);
- **withdraw your consent** to our collecting and sharing it;
- **have it deleted;**
- **see and change** your health details.

**In the app.** Your health details are in Settings, under Health details and Health & safety, where you can see and change them. While your access is paused, or a parent's or guardian's agreement is awaited, you can see them but not change them in the app (while a parent's or guardian's agreement is awaited, you can still correct a date of birth entered by mistake, unless your coach marked you as under 18). Turning off Store health details in Settings withdraws your consent and deletes them all, at any time. If you're under 18, your parent or guardian can also do this on your phone.

**By email, or through your coach.** For anything else, such as a copy, the list of who we've shared it with, a correction, or deleting your reasons, notes or training record, write to michal@fialafitness.com. To withdraw your consent without the app, for example if you agreed on a paper form, write to us or tell your coach, and we'll delete your health details within 15 days. If you have an account, send your email from the account's email address, so we know it's you; if we can't confirm that, we may ask for a little more information. You don't need to create an account to make a request. If you're under 18, your parent or guardian can also withdraw consent for your health details, or ask us to delete them, this way.

**When we answer.** Within 30 days of receiving your request, free of charge. We delete what you ask within that time, from the app, our database and your coach's devices, and from backup copies within six months.

**Appeals.** If we turn down a request, we'll tell you why. You can appeal by replying to our answer, or by writing to michal@fialafitness.com with "Appeal" in the subject line. Within 45 days, we'll tell you in writing what we did and why. If we still say no, we'll tell you how to file a complaint with the Washington or Nevada attorney general.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version with a new effective date and tell you by email or text message before an important change takes effect. We'll ask for your consent before we collect new kinds of health data, use it for new purposes, or share it with new kinds of recipients.
