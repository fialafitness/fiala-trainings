# Privacy Policy

**Effective [Effective date] · Last updated [Effective date]**

This policy explains what information Fiala Fitness keeps in our training app, why we keep it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. In this policy, "we", "us" and "your coach" mean Fiala Fitness and the trainer who coaches you through the app.

You can open this policy at any time from the Privacy Policy links on the sign-in screen and in Settings. If you live in Washington or Nevada, or use the app while you're there, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy also applies to you. Its link is right under this one, on the sign-in screen and in Settings.

## Who we are

Fiala Fitness is a personal training business in the United States. Your coach plans your workouts in the app, and you log them on your phone. The app is for clients aged 13 and up. For questions or requests about your information, write to michal@fialafitness.com.

## What we keep

- **Your name and contact details.** Your name, email address and phone number. You give them to your coach, or when you create an account in the app, and your coach keeps them on your client profile. If you have an account, your password goes to our sign-in provider, which stores it only in scrambled (hashed) form. The app never stores it, and your coach can't see it.
- **Your profile and plan.** Your photo, if you add one or agree to your coach adding one. The app keeps only a small copy, without the location a camera can store in a photo. The unit you log weights in (lb or kg). Your training plan or session package, and earlier ones (for example, the sessions used and when it ends), whether your access is active or paused, and whether your coach messages you by Messages or WhatsApp.
- **Your workouts.** The workouts your coach plans for you, and what you log: weights, reps, times, distances and sets done. When you finish, skip, miss or move a workout, and which sessions were in person. Exercises you swap, reorder, skip or finish early, and any reason you pick, such as "No time", "Travelling", "Sick", "Injury", "Fatigue" or "Discomfort". The notes you and your coach write on an exercise, with who wrote each note and when, and your coach's instructions.
- **Your coach's private notes.** Notes your coach writes about you and your training. They stay with your coach: the app never sends them to your phone, and your coach leaves them out of any monthly report sent to you.
- **Your health details and your time zone.** See the next two sections.
- **Your privacy choices.** Your answers to the app's questions about health details and your time zone, and when you gave each one. If you agreed to store health details on a paper form instead, that you signed it (and, if you're under 18, that your parent or guardian signed it too) and the date. If you're under 18, also that you're under 18, and your parent's or guardian's answer and when they gave it, with the name they typed if they agreed.
- **Technical information.** When your phone loads the app or connects to the companies that run it, they receive your device's internet (IP) address and basic browser details, and may keep them in their logs. Our sign-in provider also records when you sign in, and may keep the address and browser you signed in from, to keep your account secure. We don't add these to your profile.

## Your health details

Your health details are optional. They're what you see on two pages of the app:

- **Health details:** your date of birth, sex, height and body weight.
- **Health & safety:** your injuries and limits, medical conditions, medications and allergies, whether you're pregnant, your emergency contact and your doctor (as you write them, for example a name, relationship and phone number).

**You agree first.** Before any health details are stored, the app asks you once: "Store your health details?" The question lists these details and says what they're for: your coach plans your workouts around them, to keep your training safe. It covers both what you enter in the app and what you tell your coach. It says that only you and your coach can see them, that in an emergency your coach may share them with emergency services, your emergency contact or your doctor to get you help, and how to withdraw. You can tap Don't Allow or Agree. Both are equally easy, and nothing else in the app depends on your answer. Until you agree, nothing new is stored, and your coach can't add or change your health details. Once you've agreed, you and your coach can both enter them. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian agrees too (see "Clients under 18").

**If you don't use the app.** If you train with your coach in person and don't use the app, you can agree on a paper form instead, which says the same as the question in the app. Your coach keeps the signed form and records in the app that you signed it, and the date. Only then can your coach add or change your health details.

**If you tap Don't Allow,** no health details are kept, and any already saved are deleted. Everything else in the app works the same.

**Health details saved before we started asking.** If health details were saved for you before [Effective date], they stay until you answer: the app asks you the next time you open it (if your access is paused, once it's active again) whether to keep them, or you can answer on a paper form. Until then, your coach can still see them, but no one can change them. If you agree, they stay. If you tap Don't Allow, they're deleted.

**You can change your mind at any time.** In the app, turn off Store health details in Settings, which is as easy as agreeing: your health details are deleted at once (if your phone is offline, as soon as it's back online and you're signed in). You can also tell your coach or write to michal@fialafitness.com, and we'll delete them within 15 days. They then stay off until you agree again. We keep a record of your answers, and when you gave each one, with your account (if your phone is offline, only your last answer reaches us when it's back online, except that when your last answer is Allow or Agree, the last Don't Allow you gave before it is sent first: it counts, deletes what it covers, and stays in the record, marked as taken back; an answer we can't accept, for example an Allow given while your access is paused, changes nothing and isn't kept; if you change your answers very many times, we keep the first answer we received and the 199 most recent).

**How we use them.** To keep your training safe: your coach plans your workouts around them, sees your injuries and limits at the top of your workouts and your age under your name, and can call your emergency contact from the app if something happens. And from your date of birth, the app knows whether you're under 18 (so a parent or guardian agrees too) or under 13 (the app is for ages 13 and up). We use them for nothing else.

**Other health information in your workouts.** Some reasons you can pick also say something about your health: "Sick" or "Injury" when you skip a workout, and "Fatigue" or "Discomfort" when you skip an exercise or finish it early. So can a note on an exercise, such as "knee sore on the last set". Giving a reason or writing a note is always optional. These are part of your workout log, not your health details: the agreement above doesn't cover them, and turning health details off doesn't delete them. Your Progress page's Attendance card lists the reasons you gave for skipping workouts (you and your coach see it). While your access is active and your plan hasn't ended, you can delete a note yourself (otherwise, ask us), and you can ask us to delete a reason (see "See, correct or delete your information"). Like your health details, they're never sold or used for ads. Your coach's Progress page points out an exercise you cut short for discomfort on two or more days within four weeks, and your monthly report can show the reasons you gave for cutting an exercise short, and your exercise notes.

**Who sees them.** Only you and your coach can see your health details in the app. They're stored with our database provider, on your phone and your coach's devices, and in backup copies (see "Who else handles it"), and are never sold or used for ads. We share them with no one else, except with emergency services, your emergency contact or your doctor when it's needed to get you help in an emergency, when the law requires it, or with your written permission.

We're a personal training business, not a doctor's office or health plan, so the U.S. medical-privacy law HIPAA doesn't apply to the app. We still treat your health details as sensitive and protect them as this policy describes.

## Your time zone

The app reads your phone's time-zone setting (for example, "Asia/Tokyo"). It doesn't use GPS, ask for your location, or learn your address. A time zone covers a wide area and is named after one of its cities, which may not be where you are: all of Japan is "Asia/Tokyo", so someone in Osaka shows as "Tokyo".

- **Each finished workout** is saved with the time zone it was finished in, only so that it's filed under the day you actually did it. A Tuesday workout you finish at 8 AM in Paris stays on Tuesday, even on a screen where it's still Monday. The workout shows the date and time on that clock, never the place. When your coach marks a workout finished for you, the app may use the time zone of a workout you finished in the past week, for the same reason. Your workout log needs this, so it can't be turned off.
- **Sharing your time zone with your coach is your choice.** The app asks you once: "Share your time zone with your coach?", with the choices Don't Allow and Allow. Nothing is sent for this until you tap Allow. If you allow it, your phone sends its current time zone while the app is open on your screen: when it changes, and otherwise about every 30 minutes. We note when it arrived. Your coach then sees, under your name, that time zone's city and, when your clock differs from theirs, your local time (for example, Tokyo and Tue 3:00 AM), or, if the last one arrived more than a day ago, how long ago instead, such as "3 days ago". You see the same city in Settings in your own app. While your access is paused, your phone sends nothing: your coach keeps seeing the last one.
- **You can change your mind at any time** with the Share time zone with coach switch in Settings (while your access is paused, you can turn sharing off but not on). If you tap Don't Allow, or turn sharing off later, the app stops sending your time zone and deletes the last one saved. Your choice, and when you made it, is saved with your account, so an update or a new phone won't change it. Everything else in the app works the same either way.
- **Before we started asking.** From November 18, 2026, until [Effective date], the app sent clients' time zones without asking first. On [Effective date], we deleted the time zones saved that way for every client who hadn't tapped Allow, so your coach sees yours again only if you tap Allow. The time zone kept with each finished workout wasn't affected.
- **We keep only your latest shared time zone** (each one replaces the last), plus the one saved with each finished workout. Only you and your coach see your shared time zone in the app. Both are stored with our database provider and in the copies described under "Who else handles it", and are never sold or used for ads.

## Clients under 18

The app is for ages 13 and up, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If a date of birth under 13 is entered, the app doesn't save it, and asks the person to have a parent or guardian talk to their coach. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us information through the app, we'll pause their access and delete the information collected from them. If you think a child under 13 is using the app, please tell us at michal@fialafitness.com.

If you're 13 to 17, all of this policy applies to you, and:

- **We note on your account that you're under 18.** Your coach marks it, or the app works it out from your date of birth. If your health details are deleted while you're under 18, we keep only the year you turn 18 (not your date of birth), so the app still asks a parent or guardian until that year ends. Turning 18 doesn't change anything you agreed to.
- **Your health details need two agreements: yours, then a parent's or guardian's.** After you tap Agree, the app asks your parent or guardian, on your phone, to type their name and tap Agree too. Until you've both agreed, nothing new is stored, and your coach can't add or change your health details. If you tap Don't Allow, or your parent or guardian taps Disagree, no health details are kept, and any already saved are deleted. If we learn that you're under 18 only after you've agreed, for example from the date of birth you enter, the app asks your parent or guardian the next time you open it (if your access is paused, once it's active again), and nothing new is stored until they agree (except that a date of birth entered by mistake can still be put right, unless your coach marked you as under 18). If you agree on a paper form, you both sign it; if we learn that you're under 18 after you signed it alone, nothing new is stored until your parent or guardian signs it too or agrees in the app (except that a date of birth entered by mistake can still be put right, unless your coach marked you as under 18).
- **Either of you can change your mind.** Turn off Store health details in Settings on your phone, which deletes your health details, or tell your coach or write to michal@fialafitness.com.
- **Your time zone is your choice.** The app asks you, not your parent or guardian.
- **Your health details stay between you and your coach.** Your parent or guardian has no access of their own to your account or your health details in the app. We keep their name and answer only as a record of their agreement, and your coach sees them on your client card. We don't show or send your health details to anyone, a parent or guardian included, without your written permission, unless the law requires it or it's needed to get you help in an emergency.
- **We use your information only to coach you.** We never sell it, use it for ads, or use it to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we keep it only as long as this policy says.
- **You can remove what you've entered.** In the app, you can change your health details, clear any Health & safety entry, or turn off Store health details to delete them all; delete a note on an exercise; clear a weight or reps you logged; and remove your photo. While your access is paused, your health details can't be changed in the app: turn off Store health details to delete them all, or ask us. While your parent's or guardian's agreement is awaited, they can't be changed either, except a date of birth entered by mistake (unless your coach marked you as under 18): turn off Store health details, or ask us to delete them all, and change a single detail once they agree. While your access is paused or your plan has ended, the app doesn't show your workouts: ask us to remove something from them. For anything else you've entered, such as a reason you gave for skipping a workout, write to michal@fialafitness.com and we'll remove it. Removing something doesn't guarantee that every copy is gone at once: copies can stay in backups for up to six months, and a monthly report your coach already sent you keeps what it showed.

## How we use your information

We use it only to provide your coaching:

- to plan your workouts, let you log them, and file each one under the right day;
- to show you and your coach your progress (for example attendance, your best lifts and the lifts getting stronger), and to make a monthly report of your workouts that your coach may send you;
- to keep your training safe (see "Your health details");
- if you allow it, to show your coach your time zone's city and your local time, so your coach can plan around your day;
- to ask for your choices and keep a record of them, and to know when a client is under 18, so the app also asks a parent or guardian;
- to let you and your coach message each other by text or WhatsApp, using the phone numbers in the app;
- to run your account: signing in, confirming your email address and resetting your password;
- to keep the app secure and working, including backups.

We never sell your information, rent it, or share it for advertising. The app shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics tools. We don't use your information to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we don't collect, use or sell it to train large language models (the kind of AI behind chatbots).

## Who else handles it

A few companies run parts of the app for us:

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in and photo storage) stores everything in the app: your account, profile, health details, workouts, notes, photo, time zone and privacy choices. It also sends the emails that confirm your address and reset your password. Your information is stored in the United States.
- **GitHub** (GitHub Pages) hosts the app's files. When your phone loads the app, GitHub receives your device's internet address and browser details, but not your account or workouts.
- **Google Fonts** delivers the app's typeface, and **jsDelivr** delivers a piece of code the app uses to talk to the database. Your phone fetches these when you open the app, so both receive your device's internet address and browser details.
- **YouTube** (part of Google) plays exercise videos. Nothing loads from YouTube until you tap an exercise's play button. The video player then opens in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube receives your device's internet address, browser details and which video it is. Once you play the video, YouTube may keep its own cookies or similar data in your browser.
- **Messages or WhatsApp.** The Message button opens Messages, or WhatsApp through its own wa.me link. Your conversation happens there, under that app's privacy terms. Our app doesn't see or store it.

Your coach's app keeps a copy of your information on your coach's own devices, so it works without a signal. Your coach may also download backup copies of the app's data to their own computer, to restore it if something goes wrong. If your coach sends you your monthly report, for example by email, your coach's email or messaging service carries it.

Apart from these, we share your information only if you ask us to, if the law requires it (for example, a court order), or to get you help in an emergency.

## What's stored on your phone

So the app works without a signal, it keeps some things on your phone, in your browser's storage for this app: a copy of your workouts and profile (your health details and privacy choices included), changes waiting to be sent, your sign-in and email address (so you stay signed in), your photo and the app's own files. A few settings, such as your Appearance choice and the page you had open, are kept there too. The app itself sets no cookies.

Signing out removes your workouts, profile, photo, sign-in and email address from that phone. The app's own files and those few settings stay. If you share a phone, sign out when you're done.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account, profile, workouts, notes and photo** are kept until you ask us to delete them, including after you stop training with us, so your history is still there: your coach can see it, and so can you, in the app while your access is active and your plan hasn't ended, or by asking us for a copy.
- **Your health details** are kept until you withdraw your agreement or ask us to delete them. They're deleted as soon as you tap Don't Allow or turn off Store health details (if you're under 18, also as soon as your parent or guardian taps Disagree or turns it off), and within 15 days of telling your coach or writing to us.
- **Your shared time zone:** only the latest one, deleted when you stop sharing. **The time zone saved with a finished workout** is kept as long as that workout.
- **The record of your privacy choices,** and the paper form if you signed one, are kept until your information is deleted.
- **The copy on your phone** stays until you sign out.
- **Backups:** deleted information can remain in backup copies (our database provider's and your coach's) for up to six months, until those copies are replaced. If we ever restore one, we delete again what you asked us to delete. When your coach restores the app from one of their copies, what you deleted in the app since stays deleted, unless it was in a workout, an exercise or an account that had been deleted too and comes back from the copy. Restoring our database provider's copy could bring back anything deleted after that copy was made. Either way, we tell you and delete it again.
- **An account that isn't linked to a client profile:** if you create an account but don't train with us, we keep only your sign-in account, with the name, email address and phone number you gave, and delete it when you ask.

## See, correct or delete your information

- **See it.** Your workouts, exercise notes, progress, profile, health details and photo are in the app (your workouts, notes and progress while your access is active and your plan hasn't ended). For a copy of everything we keep about you, including what we've worked out from it (such as your age), write to michal@fialafitness.com. We'll send it in a common file format you can take elsewhere.

## How we keep it safe

- Each client can reach only their own information. The database enforces this itself: it checks who is signed in on every request, clients never read the database tables directly, and your coach's private notes are removed before your information reaches your phone.
- You sign in with your email address and a password of at least 8 characters, and you must confirm your email address before you can see anything.
- Photos are kept in a private storage folder that only you and your coach can open.
- Your information travels to and from the database over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- The database code downloaded from jsDelivr is checked against a fixed fingerprint, so a changed copy won't run.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your information, we'll tell you promptly, as the law requires.

## Do Not Track and tracking across sites

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. The app doesn't track you across other websites or apps and has no advertising or analytics tools, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends that signal.

Google (Fonts and YouTube), jsDelivr and GitHub serve many websites, so they may see your device's internet address on other sites too. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with it. Apart from the companies named in this policy, no one collects information through the app.

## If you live in the EU, EEA or UK

We don't offer coaching through the app to people who live in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom yet. If you live there, or you're moving there, please write to michal@fialafitness.com before you create an account or keep using the app, so we can check whether we can coach you and what we'd need to put in place first. We'll update this policy before we coach anyone who lives there. If you live in the United States and you're only travelling, you can keep using the app as usual.
